Think of LSC as four helpers working together. You use the web app (the published /exec link). Behind the scenes, the account that deployed the app reads and writes a Google Spreadsheet, stores photos/receipts in Drive, and sends email via Gmail.

Web app

Screens you tap: Dashboard, Members, Staff, Expenses, Calendar, Settings. Runs in the browser / phone.

Google Sheets

The gym’s notebook: members, payments, staff, salary, expenses, settings, users, dropdowns.

Google Drive

Gym logo, member photos, expense receipts — linked by file ID, not stored inside the sheet cells.

Gmail + triggers

Login codes, payment receipts, weekly due/overdue reports. Needs Mail authorization from Setup.

Simple flow

lightbulb Tip — “Execute as Me”

The app runs as the deployer’s Google account for Sheets/Drive/Mail. Visitors still sign in with Google, but the app may not see their email (common for consumer Gmail). That is why invitees use a login code or invite link — see Access below.

  1. Open the shared web app URL ending in /exec (not the editor or /dev).
  2. Sign in with Google when prompted.
  3. Go to Settings → General → Setup & Authorization. Tap Authorize Google services. Status chips cover Spreadsheet, Settings, Drive, Mail, ScriptApp, and the weekly report trigger.
  4. When checks look good, tap Mark setup complete.
  5. Fill gym name, owner name/email, and optional logo (LSC badge is used if none).
  6. Add other staff from the Users tab. Choose what each person can open and tick Email them their access link; they receive a short email with a button to open the app.
Authorization and “mark complete” are meant for the owner / deployer. You do not need the Apps Script code editor for day-to-day setup.

There is no password or code. Each user gets a personal link. Opening it signs them in, and this device remembers them.

Email

When you save a new user, they get an email saying you have given them access, with a button to open the app and the list of pages they can use. Use the mail icon on a user to send it again.

Link / QR

The QR icon on a user shows their link and QR code to copy or share. New link cancels the old one, for example if it was forwarded by mistake. Disabling or deleting a user also stops their link.

Role presets (starting point — can customize per module)

  • Admin — full View / Create / Edit / Delete, including Settings.
  • Manager — day-to-day ops (members, staff, expenses, etc.); typically no Settings edit.
  • Viewer — look around; cannot create/edit/delete; Settings usually hidden.

Menus and Save buttons hide when denied. The server also checks every action, so changing the URL alone cannot bypass permissions.

Pick a year, then monthly or quarterly, then which months/quarters. Most money totals follow that selection. Tap the small info icon on any card or chart for a short tip.

Always “now” (not fully period-filtered): Active Members, staff count chips on that card, Overdue Amount KPI, Members by Batch chart, and the Overdue / Upcoming tables.

Active Members

Count of members currently Active (not inactive / exited). Plan chips show how that count splits by membership plan.

Net-In Hand

Actual = collections recognized − expenses for elapsed periods
Remaining = forecast collections − forecast expenses for later periods
Projected total = Actual + Remaining

The card shows Actual plus a Remaining badge. The chart summary bar shows Actual / Remaining / Total. Future periods use prepaid recognition (important in Split mode), expected renewals from next due dates (skip overdue, ad-hoc, trial), trailing expense averages, and active staff salary run-rate.

Total Collected

Sum of recognized member revenue for the selected year/periods. Only payments with status Paid or Completed count. Anchor vs Split (Settings) changes which month gets the money.

Overdue Amount

Estimated unpaid dues for active members whose renewal is past due (for the selected year). Not reshaped by Anchor/Split.

Operating Expenses

Total OpEx = utilities/expense categories + paid staff costs for the periods
Utilities = Total − Staff portion

Charts at a glance

  • Revenue vs Expenses — bars of recognized revenue and expenses per period.
  • Members by Batch — active members grouped by batch.
  • Net-In-Hand Prediction — area chart; solid = actual, dotted = forecast.
  • Collection Trend — collected vs overdue series (chart type toggle).
  • Expense Breakdown — donut by category (+ staff); misc details available.
  • Staff Cost Trend — paid staff costs over periods; mini cards for headcount / period cost / average.

On Overdue and Upcoming tables, Notify / WA opens a preview with a editable message (gym, member, plan, amount, due date). Copy it or open WhatsApp via wa.me. Needs a phone on the member. No WhatsApp Business API — you send from the WhatsApp app yourself.

Overdue table: active members past due (not ad-hoc/trial). Upcoming: due within about 0–7 days, same exclusions.

Add and edit members (name, 10-digit phone, email, gender, DOB, join/exit, status, plan, batch, amount, photo). New IDs look like LSC-MEM-1, LSC-MEM-2, …

  • Filters — status (All / Active / Due Soon / Overdue), batch, plan, and search; selections stay visible while filtering.
  • Payments — record payments anytime; optional initial payment when adding. Coverage length follows the plan (e.g. monthly +1 month, quarterly +3, yearly +1 year, trial +7 days, ad-hoc +1 day).
  • Next due — usually day after latest paid coverage end; otherwise from plan frequency / join. Ad-hoc & trial show N/A for renewal due.
  • Trial fee — daily rate × inclusive days between join and exit; recalculates when plan or dates change.
  • Ad-hoc / trial exit — exit date required; when that date arrives, status becomes Inactive (on load and by a daily check).
  • Insights — per-member earnings charts; only Paid/Completed payments count as collected.
  • Photo — stored in Drive and linked on the member row.

Manage staff profiles and salary/credit transactions. There is no attendance module — payroll is about recorded payments.

  • KPIs — active headcount, total paid cost for selected periods, and Action Needed count.
  • Salary cycles — from join through today (or exit). First cycle is join→end of that month; later cycles are full calendar months. Expected amount is the full nominal monthly salary (not mid-month prorated).
  • Action Needed — a cycle with no matching paid salary row yet.
  • Credit types — Salary, Bonus, Advance, Incentive, Reimbursement. Bonuses/extras are not used to “close” a salary cycle the same way a Salary payment is.
  • Charts — payroll trend and cost-by-role donut.

Log facility and operating expenses by category and date. Optional receipt images go to Drive.

  • Misc / Other — when the category name looks like misc/other, a detail field is required.
  • KPIs — total spend (expenses + staff cost), staff cost portion, most-spent category, average per selected period.
  • Staff cost link — salary totals are shown with expenses for a full picture, but salary rows live in the Staff module (not duplicated as expense rows). The Staff Cost card can jump to Staff.
  • Charts — spend trend, category donut, misc breakdown.

Month grid of upcoming subscription renewals. Year dropdown, Today, and prev/next month controls. Tap a day for the member drawer; search the side list.

  • KPIs — Due Soon (≤2 days), In 3–5, In 6–10, Total shown.
  • Who appears — active non-ad-hoc/trial members with a next due in the month or within about 10 days.
  • Next due — usually day after latest payment end date; otherwise from paid/join + plan length. Failed/pending/cancelled payments are skipped.
  • Year list matches Dashboard / Members / Staff / Expenses (includes current year even with no data).

Guide (this page)

Living product walkthrough. No Save button — it is documentation inside the app.

Anchor (cash-style)

The whole payment amount is recognized in the start / paid month. Simple cash view.

Split (accrual-style)

Amount is spread day-by-day across the coverage window, so each calendar month gets its share. Affects Dashboard, member insights, and staff/expense money views.

What counts as collected

Only payment (and salary) statuses that mean Paid or Completed. Overdue, unpaid, pending, and failed stay visible in history but do not inflate “collected” KPIs.

Currency display

  • Indian — commas + Lakhs / Crore style.
  • Standard — compact k / M / B style.
  • Chart point labels always use compact metric so bars stay readable; tooltips and KPI cards follow your choice.

Turn the master switch on in Settings → General. Mail must be authorized in Setup.

Payment receipt

When a new payment is recorded as Paid/Completed and the member has an email, a receipt can be sent. Templates are editable with placeholders like member name, plan, amount, dates.

Weekly report

Scheduled email to the owner (plus CC list) with upcoming and overdue members. Choose day, time, days-ahead window, and which table columns appear. Preview samples from Settings.

Note: the weekly report’s “due” uses payment end dates; the Calendar screen’s next due is usually end date + 1 day. Both are intentional for their jobs.

  • Form validation — Save checks required fields; highlights errors. Conditional fields (misc details, exit date, salary coverage, initial payment) validate only when shown.
  • Lazy lists — Members, Staff, and Expenses load in batches of 50; scroll near the bottom for more. Search/filter resets to the first 50 matches.
  • Dates — shown as day-month-year style (e.g. 01-Oct-2026).
  • Progress & toasts — longer actions show a progress overlay; short feedback appears as toast messages.
  • Modals on mobile — dialogs and sheets stay above the bottom navigation so Save/Cancel and login actions are never hidden under the nav; tall panels scroll inside the safe area.
  • Small screens — text scales down a bit on narrow phones; the page should not scroll sideways. Wide tables scroll inside their box instead.
  • Charts — shared styling and mobile-friendly zoom/pan toolbars across modules.
  • Stable web URL — keep one published /exec link. Redeploying should update that same deployment so QR codes and bookmarks do not break.
  • Local env IDs — developers store spreadsheet, Drive, script, and deployment IDs in a local config/env.json (not committed). Copy from config/env.example.json.
  • Mock data — a generator can wipe and refill sample members/payments/staff/expenses for testing. It is for development only; do not run it on a live gym’s real data.
  • Handover — client should own Spreadsheet, Apps Script, and Drive; create the web app deployment as themselves; authorize from Settings; then invite staff.
